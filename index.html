Interactive survey prototype

Predict the treatment effects

Six periods. Three treatment–control comparisons. Enter your predictions directly on the graph or in the table.

Demonstration only. Axis limits −0.10 to +0.30 are examples. Responses stay on this page and are not sent anywhere.

Please predict the difference between each treatment arm and the Control arm for each of the six individual two-week periods.

For consumption, enter log points. For example, 0.10 means 0.10 log points higher consumption than Control (approximately 10.5% higher). Zero means no difference.
